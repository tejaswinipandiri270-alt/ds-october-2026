PASSWORD RESET AND MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION
This is fictional training guidance, not an actual company policy. Use the organisation-approved self-service password reset portal from a trusted device. Verify identity with registered MFA and choose a unique password that meets policy. Never share a password or one-time code, including with someone claiming to be IT. If reset fails, contact the IT Service Desk through the approved internal route with username, device name, exact error and time. For lost or changed phones, request identity verification and MFA re-enrolment. Never approve an MFA request you did not initiate.
