PURPOSE
This guide explains how employees can reset their company account password and resolve common multi-factor authentication (MFA) issues.

PASSWORD RESET
1. Open the company sign-in page from the approved employee portal.
2. Select "Forgot password".
3. Enter your registered work email address.
4. Complete the identity verification steps shown on screen.
5. Create a new password that meets the company's password rules.
6. Sign in again using the new password.

PASSWORD RULES
- Use at least 14 characters.
- Do not reuse any of your previous 10 passwords.
- Do not use your name, work email, or common dictionary phrases.
- Never share your password with another person or enter it into an unverified link.

MFA TROUBLESHOOTING
- If the authenticator app shows an incorrect code, check that the phone date and time are set automatically.
- If you changed or lost your phone, contact the IT Service Desk for identity verification and MFA re-registration.
- If you receive an MFA approval request that you did not initiate, deny it and report it to the IT Service Desk immediately.
- Do not approve repeated prompts that you did not initiate.

WHEN TO ESCALATE
Contact the IT Service Desk if the self-service reset fails, your account is locked for more than 15 minutes, or you suspect someone else has accessed your account.
