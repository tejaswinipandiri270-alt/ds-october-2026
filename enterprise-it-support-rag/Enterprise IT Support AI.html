How do I access Outlook?

smart_toy

To access Outlook, you should ensure that your internet connection is stable and that your device is connected to the company's network. Here are the steps you can follow:

  1. Open your web browser.
  2. Type the following URL into the address bar: https://outlook.office365.com/
  3. Click "Sign in" and enter your work email address and password.
  4. Confirm your identity by entering the code sent to your email or through the authenticator app.

If you are still having issues, you can refer to the troubleshooting section in the document [Source: 03_email_and_outlook.txt].

VPN AND REMOTE ACCESS Use the company-approved VPN client on a trusted network. Sign in with the corporate account and complete MFA.
