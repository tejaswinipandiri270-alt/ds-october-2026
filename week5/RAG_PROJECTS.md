# RAG projects on AWS: one per person

Each person builds a **RAG application for a different business** and deploys it on AWS.
**Deadline and presentation: Tuesday 17 November 2026.**

RAG is the only thing to build now. Each project could later grow into single- and multi-agent systems: describe those ideas in your README, but don't build them yet.

## What everyone builds

```
documents ─► S3 ─► ingestion job (chunk + embed) ─► Amazon OpenSearch (k-NN index + metadata)
                                                           ▲
user ─► FastAPI /query (Docker image in ECR, running on ECS Fargate) ─┘ ─► Azure OpenAI ─► answer + citations
                     secrets (Azure OpenAI key, OpenSearch credentials) from AWS Secrets Manager
```

| # | Requirement |
|---|---|
| 1 | 20+ source documents (synthetic or public) uploaded to an S3 bucket |
| 2 | Ingestion script: read from S3, chunk, embed, write to an Amazon OpenSearch k-NN index with metadata |
| 3 | Labelled evaluation set: 30+ questions with the correct source chunk(s), incl. 5 the documents can't answer |
| 4 | FastAPI: GET /health, POST /query (Pydantic in/out: answer, citations, scores), 'I don't know' below a threshold |
| 5 | Dockerfile; image builds and runs locally |
| 6 | Image pushed to Amazon ECR |
| 7 | Service running on Amazon ECS (Fargate), settings via env vars, IAM role for S3/OpenSearch (no keys in code) |
| 8 | Azure OpenAI key and OpenSearch credentials stored in AWS Secrets Manager and injected into the ECS task |
| 9 | Your metrics computed and reported in the README, with one change you made and its before/after effect |
| 10 | README: architecture diagram, how to run, .env.example, 'Future: agents' section (ideas only) |
| 11 | Tear down OpenSearch / ECS when not in use (they cost money while running) |
| 12 | Repo link added as a comment on this card |

**Documents** can be synthetic (written or generated by you) or from public sources. Never use real personal or confidential data.

### Akinlolu Oyetakin · Aircraft maintenance manual assistant

**Business scenario:** An aircraft maintenance (MRO) company: technicians on the hangar floor need the exact procedure, torque values and part numbers for a task, fast, for the right aircraft type and the latest manual revision.

**Documents:** Maintenance procedures for 2–3 aircraft types, with chapters, task numbers, numbered steps, warnings, part numbers and revision dates. Synthetic, or based on the public FAA Aviation Maintenance Technician Handbooks (faa.gov).

**Chunking:** **Structure-aware, hierarchical:** chunk by task/section and never split a numbered procedure or a WARNING from its step. Keep aircraft type, chapter, task number and revision date as metadata.

**Retrieval:** **Hybrid (BM25 + vectors)** in OpenSearch, because part numbers and task codes need exact matches; **metadata filter** on aircraft type; only the **latest revision** may be returned.

**Metrics:** Recall@5 on the labelled set; **exact-match accuracy of part numbers and torque values** in answers; **step completeness** (share of procedure steps included); % of answers citing an outdated revision (target 0).

**Example questions:**
- What is the torque for the main wheel axle nut on the A-200?
- List the steps to replace the cabin pressure sensor (latest revision).

**Future enhancement (README only):** A work-order agent that drafts the maintenance task card; later, agents for parts inventory and hangar scheduling.

### Saraswathi Lakshman · Home insurance claims coverage assistant

**Business scenario:** An insurer's claims handlers must decide quickly whether a claim is covered under the customer's policy tier and version, without missing an exclusion.

**Documents:** Home insurance policy wordings for 3 tiers (Basic, Standard, Premium) and 2 versions, with definitions, cover sections, exclusions and limits. Synthetic, or modelled on public policy wordings from insurers' websites.

**Chunking:** **Parent–child (small-to-big):** index small clause-level chunks for precise matching, but send the whole parent section to the LLM. Definitions become their own chunks, linked to the clauses that use them.

**Retrieval:** **Metadata filter** on tier and version, then **multi-query retrieval** (one query for cover, one for exclusions and limits), then **re-ranking** with a cross-encoder.

**Metrics:** Precision@5; **context recall for exclusions** (was the relevant exclusion retrieved whenever one applies?); **coverage decision accuracy** (covered / not covered / refer to a human) vs your labels; faithfulness scored by an LLM judge.

**Example questions:**
- A pipe burst while the house was empty for 45 days. Is it covered on Standard v2?
- Is accidental damage to a laptop covered on Basic?

**Future enhancement (README only):** A claims-triage agent that requests missing evidence; later, multi-agent with fraud checks and payout calculation.

### Tejaswini Pandiri · Supplier contract intelligence assistant

**Business scenario:** A procurement team manages dozens of supplier contracts and keeps missing renewal deadlines, SLA penalties and notice periods hidden in the clauses.

**Documents:** 30+ supplier contracts (IT, facilities, logistics) with parties, start/end dates, value, auto-renewal, notice period and SLA tables. Synthetic, or based on public templates such as the UK Government Model Services Contract.

**Chunking:** **Clause-based** chunking, with **SLA tables kept whole as separate chunks**; contract-level fields (supplier, category, start/end date, value, auto-renew) extracted once and attached to every chunk as metadata.

**Retrieval:** **Self-query:** turn the question into metadata filters (supplier, date range, category) plus a semantic query. Questions across contracts ("which contracts renew in Q1?") must use metadata, not just top-k.

**Metrics:** MRR; **exact-match accuracy on dates, amounts and notice periods**; **list recall** for multi-contract questions (did it find all qualifying contracts?); p95 latency of /query.

**Example questions:**
- What is the notice period to terminate the CleanCo facilities contract?
- Which IT contracts auto-renew before 31 March 2027?

**Future enhancement (README only):** A renewal-alert agent that emails owners before deadlines; later, multi-agent negotiation preparation.

### Helena Lawrence · Householder planning permission assistant

**Business scenario:** A local council's planning helpdesk gets the same questions from residents every day: do I need planning permission for my extension, loft conversion, fence or outbuilding?

**Documents:** Public UK guidance (e.g. gov.uk "Permitted development rights for householders: technical guidance" and the Planning Portal) plus synthetic local council policies (conservation areas, local design rules).

**Chunking:** **Semantic chunking:** split where the topic changes (based on embedding similarity between sentences), not at fixed sizes. Keep the rule, its conditions and its worked examples together.

**Retrieval:** **Query rewriting / HyDE:** residents ask in everyday words ("can I build a shed"), the guidance uses legal terms ("outbuildings, Class E"). Rewrite or expand the question before searching.

**Metrics:** Hit@3 on **two versions of every question** (resident wording vs expert wording) and the **gap between them**, before and after rewriting; **out-of-scope refusal accuracy**; answer correctness vs your labels.

**Example questions:**
- Can I put a 3-metre-deep extension on the back of my semi-detached house?
- Do I need permission for a shed at the bottom of my garden?

**Future enhancement (README only):** An agent that checks a described plan against each rule and drafts the application checklist; later, multi-agent with a resident-facing assistant and a compliance checker.

### Toni Miran Borders · Vulnerability advisory assistant (security)

**Business scenario:** A company's security team must answer "are we affected, how bad is it, and what's the fix?" for new vulnerabilities in the software they run, before attackers exploit them.

**Documents:** Public vulnerability records: CVE entries from the NVD (nvd.nist.gov), the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog and vendor advisories, for a chosen set of products. Plus a synthetic list of the company's software and versions.

**Chunking:** **Record-based:** one chunk per CVE / advisory, with CVE ID, product, affected versions, CVSS score, date and "known exploited" as metadata; split only long advisories, by section.

**Retrieval:** **Hybrid with exact matching** on CVE IDs and product/version, **recency boosting** (newer advisories rank higher), and filters on severity and "known exploited".

**Metrics:** **nDCG@10 with graded relevance** (exact product + version = 2, same product = 1, other = 0); Recall@10; **freshness** (share of answers citing the newest advisory); version-match accuracy in answers.

**Example questions:**
- Is OpenSSL 3.0.7 affected by any known exploited vulnerability?
- What is the fix for CVE-2024-3094 and which versions are affected?

**Future enhancement (README only):** An agent that scans the asset inventory and opens tickets for affected systems; later, multi-agent triage, patch planning and reporting.
